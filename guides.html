Discipline: a decision checklist

  1. Write down what you expect and what would invalidate it.
  2. Know your maximum acceptable loss and all costs.
  3. Check the venue, order type and custody arrangement.
  4. Record the outcome and what you learned.

A checklist supports decisions; it does not establish a profitable strategy.

The long game: keep a useful record

Separate deposits and withdrawals from gains and losses. Record fees, dates and your original reasoning. Review what actually happened rather than rewriting the thesis afterwards.
