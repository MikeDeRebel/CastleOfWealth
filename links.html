Why these checks?

Look-ahead bias, survivorship bias and overfitting can make historical strategies look more dependable than they are. Reserve unseen periods, test a negative control and account for costs before trusting a result.

MDR WEALTH VAULT / 004

A castle cannot depend on next week's market price. Before allocating capital, check what remains available for essential spending and the commitments already due.

THE REBEL RULEEssential cash is not risk capital.
  1. 01 / CommitmentsReserve the cash already owed.

What belongs in accessible cash?

Count cash you can actually use for bills without selling an investment or waiting for a withdrawal. Exclude volatile crypto assets, trading collateral, locked staking or yield, properties, unsettled sales and unused credit lines. Deduct bills that are already due or committed. This deliberately conservative definition is for this worksheet, not a judgement of asset quality.

Your reserve floor is a personal planning choice, not a recommended number. Income reliability, dependants and spending flexibility can change how much cover you want.

02 / The MDR method

A process you can stand behind.

The same discipline, from a new idea
to the next review.

  1. 01 ANALYSIS

    Understand first.

    Separate evidence from assumptions. Look for the strongest case against your idea.

    Your output
    Evidence + open questions
  2. 02 DECISION

    Make the trade-off.

    Act, wait, research more or decline. Write your reason, limits and what would change your mind.

    Your output
    A decision with boundaries
  3. 03 APPROVAL

    Keep the final say.

    Pause. Check the evidence, downside and next step yourself before committing.

    Your output
    Your deliberate approval
  4. 04 EXECUTION

    Follow your plan.

    Carry out only the step you approved. Verify the details. Pause if the conditions change.

    Your output
    A recorded action
  5. 05 REVIEW

    Let the record teach.

    Compare the outcome with your original reasoning. Keep the lesson and improve one thing.

    Your output
    A lesson + a review date

KISS One clear thesis. Defined limits. One useful record.

04 / MDR R Engine · Position-risk laboratory

Risk first.
Size second.

A stop is a plan, not a guarantee.
Calculate the downside before you decide.

MDR / MODEL 001NO ORDERS · NO SYNC

Define your 1R.

Everything here is an assumption you control. No venue fees, market prices or account balances are fetched.

Start with 1× maximum notional (no modeled leverage). Higher caps do not check liquidation, margin requirements or borrowing costs. Every fee and buffer is required, even if the correct value is zero.

How this MDR calculation works

1R budget = equity × risk %. Estimated per-unit loss = |entry − stop| + entry × entry-fee % + stop × adverse-slippage % + (stop + adverse-slippage amount) × exit-fee %. Model units = minimum of (1R / loss per unit) and (equity × maximum notional multiple / entry), rounded down to 8 decimal places. This is a planning estimate, not a guaranteed fill or a broker-approved order. It excludes funding, borrow costs, taxes, gaps beyond your buffer and liquidation conditions.
