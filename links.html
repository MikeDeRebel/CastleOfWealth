02 / The MDR method

A process you can stand behind.

The same discipline, from a new idea
to the next review.

  1. 01 ANALYSIS

    Understand first.

    Separate evidence from assumptions. Look for the strongest case against your idea.

    Your output
    Evidence + open questions
  2. 02 DECISION

    Make the trade-off.

    Act, wait, research more or decline. Write your reason, limits and what would change your mind.

    Your output
    A decision with boundaries
  3. 03 APPROVAL

    Keep the final say.

    Pause. Check the evidence, downside and next step yourself before committing.

    Your output
    Your deliberate approval
  4. 04 EXECUTION

    Follow your plan.

    Carry out only the step you approved. Verify the details. Pause if the conditions change.

    Your output
    A recorded action
  5. 05 REVIEW

    Let the record teach.

    Compare the outcome with your original reasoning. Keep the lesson and improve one thing.

    Your output
    A lesson + a review date

KISS One clear thesis. Defined limits. One useful record.

04 / MDR R Engine · Position-risk laboratory

Risk first.
Size second.

A stop is a plan, not a guarantee.
Calculate the downside before you decide.

MDR / MODEL 001NO ORDERS · NO SYNC

Define your 1R.

Everything here is an assumption you control. No venue fees, market prices or account balances are fetched.

Start with 1× maximum notional (no modeled leverage). Higher caps do not check liquidation, margin requirements or borrowing costs. Every fee and buffer is required, even if the correct value is zero.

Record your reasoning ↗
RISK MODEL / OUTPUTSTAND DOWN

Enter your equity, planned entry and stop, risk percentage, both fees and a slippage buffer to unlock the model.

How this MDR calculation works

1R budget = equity × risk %. Estimated per-unit loss = |entry − stop| + entry × entry-fee % + stop × exit-fee % + stop × adverse-slippage %. Model units = minimum of (1R / loss per unit) and (equity × maximum notional multiple / entry), rounded down to 8 decimal places. This is a planning estimate, not a guaranteed fill or a broker-approved order. It excludes funding, borrow costs, taxes, gaps beyond your buffer and liquidation conditions.
